Frame & Score

Anime, film & music, decoded.

Frame & Score is an independent creator studio that publishes original short videos and posts about anime, film and music culture on YouTube, TikTok and X. Content is produced with AI assistance and reviewed by a human before publishing.

Frame & Score Studio (publishing tool)

Frame & Score Studio is the studio's publishing tool. It renders the studio's original vertical videos and hands them to the creator's own accounts as drafts, so a human always reviews and publishes each video manually.

  • TikTok: the creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the video.upload permission. The tool then sends finished videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API). The creator opens TikTok, edits the caption and posts it themselves. The tool never posts publicly on its own.
  • YouTube: videos are uploaded to the creator's own channel as private videos for review.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their TikTok account on the authorization page.
  2. The studio renders an original video (script, narration, licensed stock footage).
  3. The tool uploads it to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. The creator receives a TikTok notification, reviews the draft and publishes it in the TikTok app.
