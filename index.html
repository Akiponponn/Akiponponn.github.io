Frame & Score

Anime, film & music, decoded.

Frame & Score is an independent channel that publishes original short videos and posts about anime, film and music culture on YouTube, TikTok and X. Content is produced with AI assistance and reviewed by the channel owner before publishing.

About this site

This site describes the small internal tool the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube as private drafts. The tool is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.
