Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers Frame & Score Studio ("the tool"), the publishing tool of the Frame & Score studio, which sends the studio's original videos to connected creator accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

TikTok

  • The tool requests the TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.upload only.
  • video.upload is used solely to send videos produced by the studio to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The account owner reviews and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
  • user.info.basic (open ID, display name, avatar) is used only to confirm which account is connected.

YouTube

  • The tool requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload only, to upload the studio's videos to the owner's own channel as private videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool stores

  • OAuth refresh tokens for connected accounts, kept as private secrets in the studio's server environment. They are never published, sold or shared.
  • No data about viewers or other users is collected, stored, sold or shared. No analytics or advertising trackers are used on this site.

Revoking access

TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, stored tokens are deleted.
