Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the Frame & Score upload tool ("the tool"), which the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

What the tool accesses

  • The tool requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload only.
  • It uses that permission solely to upload videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel as private videos, and to set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.

What the tool stores

  • An OAuth refresh token for the owner's own account, kept as a private secret in the owner's environment. It is never published or shared.
  • No data about other YouTube users or viewers is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Use of Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
